## Part 1: Official Step-by-Step Guide to Cancel Teal+

If you are trying to cancel your Teal+ membership to prevent unexpected card deductions:

1. Log into your **Teal Web App** (do not use the Chrome extension dashboard).
2. Click on your **Profile Avatar** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Settings** -> navigate to the **Billing / Subscription** tab.
4. Click **Manage Plan** -> select **Cancel Membership**.
5. **Crucial Step**: Proceed through the 3 confirmation popups until you receive a formal cancellation email. Take a screenshot for your banking records.

⚠️ **Data Retention Warning**: Once cancelled, Teal locks premium AI resume formatting and keyword matching scores on all existing job tracker cards.
